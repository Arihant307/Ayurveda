## Trade-off

The validation FPR budget is held near 1% to keep clinicians' legitimate mail
from being flagged, at some cost in recall. At the default 0.5 threshold the selected model
scores recall 0.9896 and FPR 0.0246 on the same test set.

## Limitations

- Enron-Spam labels spam vs ham; phishing is a subset of spam, so the score means 'malicious or unsolicited', not phishing specifically. Phishing-specific signals come from the rule indicators.
- Training mail dates from 1999-2005; modern lures (QR codes, OAuth consent, MFA fatigue) are under-represented. Re-train on recent, locally labelled mail before production use.
- Header features are not model inputs because the preprocessed corpus has no headers.

## Limitations

- Collected in 2017-2018; phishing infrastructure changes quickly, so re-train on fresh feeds (PhishTank / OpenPhish / URLhaus exports) regularly.
- Legitimate URLs come from search-engine results, which are biased toward popular, content-rich sites; internal hospital portals and vendor URLs may look unusual and score higher.
- Lexical analysis cannot see page content, certificates or redirects by design.

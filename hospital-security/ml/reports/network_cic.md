## Limitations

- Lab traffic from 2018 (CIC testbed). Hospital networks, medical-device protocols (DICOM, HL7, IoMT) and modern attacks are not represented.
- The official CSVs are truncated at 1,048,575 rows per day, and heavy de-duplication leaves very few FTP brute-force (46) and SQL-injection flows; recall for those labels is unreliable.
- Infiltration flows are largely indistinguishable from benign flows at the flow level (a known property of this dataset); expect low recall for that category.
- Destination port is a strong feature; attacks on non-standard ports may be missed.
